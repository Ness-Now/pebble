## Corrected product capability

CIV-42 now owns a bounded lexical innovation transition on the existing
`AgentSimulationSession` aggregate. The public caller selects an individual
and a shared semantic sense. It cannot supply the accepted word. Product logic
requires two retained faithful local CIV-43 uses of the seeded pack form by
that individual to distinct recipients, selects the exact latest causal
supports in stable order, and derives the evolved form and decision digest
deterministically from bounded session, pack, individual, sense and support
authority.

The accepted innovation is sparse individual state. It records the innovator,
shared pack and sense, source association and form, exact local-use support
receipts, evolved form, causal event and decision digest. A learned lexical
association and every later communication or writing use retain the innovation
identity. Normal semantic realization deterministically prefers the newest
causally acquired competent association, with stable tie-breaks. The result is
therefore observable language behavior, not a label, counter, cultural
descriptor, fixture-selected string or test-only dialect flag.

The decisive history starts every relevant individual with the same pack form
`bois` for `value.resource.wood`. Local product history evolves
`vatmut-19f91ddb`; a second local individual learns and realizes that evolved
form, while the separated population continues to realize `bois` for the same
sense.

## Durable representation and schema compatibility

Lexical evolution is an optional bounded field of the existing language graph.
A session with no lexical innovation omits it and retains its historical
schema-37 through schema-42 representation and provenance semantics. The first
actual innovation promotes the checkpoint and replay schema to 43. Schema 43
requires the existing knowledge, language, oral and social authorities and
continues strict durable Family and Estate validation. Schema 44 remains
unsupported.

Restore recomputes each evolved form and digest from its product-authored
supports. It validates exact pack/sense/source binding, causal event identity,
local oral receipt, provenance digests, association roots, communication uses,
writing uses, counts and boundaries. Fabricated alternatives, form rebinding,
stale or substituted supports, substituted carriers, compacted
self-authorization and replay-injected effects fail closed.

Recorded-effect replay retains the accepted evolved form and decision digest,
then recomputes and compares them. It cannot invent or reroll a word. Duplicate
retries return the one existing individual/sense innovation without publishing
duplicate authority.

## Bounds, compaction and mortality

Innovation and association counts use the existing bounded language
configuration. Public inputs are bounded before proportional work. Candidate
state and causal append capacity are prevalidated, and N+1 refusal preserves
authoritative bytes and ordinals.

Each innovation contains exactly two bounded support receipts. A retained
local-oral authority receipt and the authenticated language provenance
boundary permit the original CIV-42 communication and CIV-43 carrier rows to
compact without pinning them forever. Dropped event bodies remain admissible
only below the committed causal boundary; current lexical associations cannot
authorize their own origin. Unreferenced innovation roots may themselves be
compacted with a monotonic eviction count.

Final mortality removes the dead individual's current lexical competence. It
does not transfer it to survivors or rewrite historical communications,
innovation events or already learned living associations. The terminal state
and its compacted history restore exactly.

## Minimality review

The product extends the one CIV-42 state and transition owner; there is no
parallel dialect subsystem or source of lexical truth. The product API adds
only the individual/sense innovation trigger. The internal accepted effect is
replay-only. The full restore validator pre-indexes innovations for bounded
whole-state checks, while the literacy path performs the same exact
innovation/sense/form relation against state; these are two access shapes over
one authority, not duplicated authority.

Most product-side expansion is the schema-43 canonical encoding, provenance
validation, adversarial rejection and compaction handoff required to keep the
new durable state reconstructible. Moving it to another file would not reduce
the authority surface. No test fixture logic or dialect flag is embedded in
product code, and no aesthetic refactor is justified after the green proof.
